## Direction contract

THESIS: A shared-money page with Japanese restraint: one calm sheet where every number answers "says who?" inline. Space does the grouping; nothing is boxed. Refuses the card-grid dashboard, stat tiles and charts.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground with a cool blue cast, white ink, muted grey-blue secondary ink. Sui blue is the only colour with intent: actions ("Why"), the next payer, "Today", and the brand dot. Soft blue for "gets back", muted coral for "owes". Section headings sit on a hairline that runs to the edge; rows are separated by hairlines only. Large figures in extra-light Geist with tight tracking; everything else Geist regular/medium. Initials in 1px outlined circles; Lucide icons at 1.25 stroke; corners 0–2px.

STORY: A judge or flatmate sees the open total and the two payments that clear it in seconds, then opens any "Why" and sees the facts, formula and threshold. Calm and exact, so nobody feels accused.
