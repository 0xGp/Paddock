# ADR-0010: Scope cut on Oct 28, 2026

- **Status:** Accepted (replaces the MVP list in SPEC §6.0)
- **Date:** 2026-10-28

## Context
On Oct 28 the repo had no code. The plan expected M0 (first Mainnet memory) on Oct 20, M1 (engine) on Oct 25 and M2 (restore round-trip) on Oct 27. There are 13 days left, including video, blog and submission. Neon, Enoki and Google OAuth aren't set up yet.

## Decision
**MVP (must ship):** R0 (basic grammar), R1, R2, R3, R5, R6, R7, R10; the memory envelope, outbox, learned-layer recall and restore with `ledgerHash` verification; Google zkLogin plus one gasless USDC settlement confirmed through Tatum GraphQL; `/demo`, `/status`, the memory timeline, the memory-off toggle, the "Verify it yourself" README, blog and video.

**Cut:** R4 payday window, R8 anomaly and missed bills, R9 nudge timing, R11 outcome learning, PWA, email, portable member profile, AI Builder.

**Stretch, only after M4:** R8 missed-bill flag (cheap, built on R2) and then R11.

## Consequences
- The video drops the "nudge timed to Emeka's payday" beat. Emeka's "slow" badge (R5) and the "Whose turn?" rotation (R3 + R6) carry the "it learned" story, together with the alias correction (R1) and the dismissal (R10).
- Revised milestones are in `docs/REVIEW-2026-10-28.md` §5.
